# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@nakodax.com** with the subject `Security: nkx-source-protection`. Include what you found,
how to reproduce it, and how to reach you. Please do not open a public issue
for a security report.

## What counts

Weaknesses in how this repository is protected, or in NakodaX products
documented here. Attempts to brute-force or otherwise break the encryption of
this repository's files are out of scope without a prior written agreement.
