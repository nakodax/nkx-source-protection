# Requesting review access

Review access to `nkx-source-protection` is granted case by case.

## Who we say yes to

Customers and prospective customers doing due diligence, investors and their
technical advisers, independent auditors, and security researchers with a
specific question.

## How it works

1. **Ask.** Email support@nakodax.com with the subject `Source review: nkx-source-protection`. Say who
   you are, who you represent, and what you want to verify.
2. **Agree terms.** We send a mutual NDA and, for audits, a short scope.
3. **Review.** We run a live walkthrough or grant time-limited access to the
   relevant material.

## Just want to see the protection work?

You do not need to go through review for that. [nkx-sample-app](https://github.com/nakodax/nkx-sample-app)
ships an encrypted application and explains how to request a licence key that
unlocks it on your own machine.
